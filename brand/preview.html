Rules

Clear space

Keep at least 0.25 × mark height of empty space on every side of the mark and lockup. Nothing enters that band — no text, borders, or other logos.

Minimum size

Mark: 20 px tall. Horizontal lockup: 96 px wide. Below that, drop the lockup and use the mark alone.

Do not

Do not recolour the brackets, swap the blue, add gradients or shadows, rotate, outline, or re-space the wordmark. One colour only via the mono variants.

Backgrounds

Two-tone on white and light greys. On dark surfaces use the inverse variants. On photography or busy colour, use logo-mono.svg in white.
